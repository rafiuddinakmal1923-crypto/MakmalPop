# MakmalPop: competition video script (task B6)

Target length **4:30** (hard limit 5:00). Phase 1 judging is on this video only, so every rubric item must show
on screen: originality (25%), real problem + ease of use (25%), impact + feasibility (25%), presentation (25%).

Narration is in Bahasa Melayu with English subtitles. *The BM lines are a first draft: have a native speaker
read them aloud before recording.*

## Filming rules
- **Airplane mode on camera, before anything else, and visible the whole time** (status bar in frame). This is
  the strongest proof of the offline claim (NFR-01).
- Use a real low-end Android phone if the team can borrow one; say its RAM on screen (for example "2 GB RAM").
- The DEMO watermark stays on (Settings → Tanda air DEMO), as the rules ask.
- No real student faces or names unless everyone filmed has agreed in writing; team members only.
- No textbook pages or photos in the video (CLAUDE.md: never copy textbook images).
- Record the screen at 1080p (Android: built-in screen recorder) and film the projector with a second phone.
- Before filming, open Settings and turn on **Buka semua halaman** so the experiments can be shown in any order.
