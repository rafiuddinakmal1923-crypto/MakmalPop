## Judging rubric (Innovation Challenge, 25% each per the site's data)
1. Originality and creativity, including fit with the Edutainment & Innovation theme
2. Effectiveness in solving a real problem, and ease of use
3. Potential impact (social, economic or environmental) and feasibility
4. Quality of the presentation and Q&A

Phase 1: online screening based on the video only. Phase 2: live pitch + Q&A for finalists.
