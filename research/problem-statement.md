# Problem statement (draft, ~590 characters for the entry form)

Many rural schools, especially in Sabah and Sarawak, still lack proper science labs and equipment, so
students learn physics and chemistry experiments only from textbooks. Research shows rural schools
struggle to run regular experiments due to limited infrastructure, equipment and safety constraints,
and many students have never used basic apparatus. Without seeing what happens, students memorise
results instead of understanding concepts and come to see science as hard. Existing digital tools often
need fast internet and good devices, which rural schools also lack.
